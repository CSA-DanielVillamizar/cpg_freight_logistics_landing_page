How quickly can we be added as an Additional Insured?

Standard turnaround time for custom Certificates of Insurance naming your general contracting firm, project owner, or municipality as additional insured (with primary & non-contributory wording and waiver of subrogation) is under 45 minutes during normal operating hours.

Does CPG qualify for Florida State and Federal DBE quotas?

Yes. CPG Enterprises is fully certified through the Florida Unified Certification Program (FLUCP) under Partner ID #484220. Our participation counts directly toward both FDOT state-funded highway projects and federal DOT DBE utilization requirements across NAICS codes 484110 and 484220.

Who manages Florida oversize and overweight road permits?

Our dedicated in-house routing and compliance dispatchers handle all state (FDOT Permit Office), county, and municipal permits directly. We calculate axle weight distributions, submit route surveys, and coordinate Florida Highway Patrol or certified civilian escort vehicles directly with no intermediary brokers.

Can we request driver qualification files (DQFs) for pre-job audits?

Yes. Complete sanitized Driver Qualification Files—including active Medical Examiner’s Certificates, annual MVR reviews, and CCO rigging certifications—are available within 60 minutes for industrial site safety orientations upon execution of freight dispatch.
