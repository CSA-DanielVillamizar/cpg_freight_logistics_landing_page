## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the commanding reliability and technical precision required of a heavy haul, oversized load, and specialized flatbed logistics leader. The aesthetic blends **Corporate Precision** with **Industrial Rigor**, projecting institutional stability, military-grade execution, and safety-first compliance.

Targeting enterprise freight brokers, industrial manufacturers, civil engineering contractors, and supply chain managers, the interface establishes instant credibility and structural confidence. The emotional response should be one of complete operational assurance: durable, immovable, punctual, and technologically advanced.

Visual hallmarks include deep structural navy surfaces, dense steel slate borders, razor-sharp edge treatments, clear hierarchical metrics, and high-visibility industrial amber accents engineered for rapid visual processing in field, dispatch, and boardroom environments.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, high-contrast hierarchy anchored by corporate industrial tones:

- **Primary (`#0B192C`)**: Deep maritime navy serving as the foundational anchor for headers, primary actions, heavy text elements, and navigation shells.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B`)**: Dark slate steel gray for structural data containers, card borders, subtle backgrounds, and secondary typography.
- **Tertiary (`#EA580C`)**: High-contrast safety amber-orange derived from industrial transport warning standards. Reserved strictly for critical calls to action, active transit alerts, load approvals, and priority interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate neutral delivering balanced mid-tone contrast for metadata, table headers, breadcrumbs, and inactive controls.

### Named Functional Colors
- **`safety-amber` (`#F59E0B`)**: Advisory status indicators, permit warnings, and load dimension notices.
- **`hazard-orange` (`#EA580C`)**: Immediate interaction triggers and critical operational alerts.
- **`fleet-blue` (`#1C3766`)**: Accent maritime tint for links, metric charts, and category tags.
- **`signal-red` (`#DD1A1A`)**: Critical failure alerts, route stoppage indicators, and over-capacity warnings.
- **`surface-light` (`#F8FAFC`) / `surface-muted` (`#F1F5F9`)**: Crisp, low-fatigue application backdrops that provide sharp definition against dark framing elements.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes a tripartite structure tailored to precision industrial interfaces:

1. **Headlines (`Chivo`)**: A contemporary grotesque typeface with masculine geometry and sharp mechanical stroke terminals. Conveys impact, momentum, and operational authority in all major display titles and hero figures.
2. **Body (`Inter`)**: A hyper-legible, utilitarian workhorse that eliminates visual fatigue across dense freight manifests, documentation, and operational summaries.
3. **Data & Technical Labels (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Monospaced clarity applied to BOL numbers, axle load metrics, GPS coordinates, freight dimensions, and equipment status badges to prevent tabular visual shift and ensure exact legibility.

All uppercase labels maintain positive letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to preserve legibility against dark slate backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an architectural 12-column fluid grid bound to a maximum container width of `80rem` (1280px) for standard workflows, with an expanded 16-column layout for real-time dispatch dashboards. 

### Breakpoints & Rhythm
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters and page margins. Controls scale to 48px minimum touch targets. Data grids collapse into vertical specification cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px margins. Side-by-side spec layouts and consolidated metadata sidebars become active.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, auto-centering container. Accommodates high-density fleet tracking tables, dual-column quotation builders, and structured technical spec layouts.

Spacing increments adhere strictly to an 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2rem`), allowing compact 4px steps only for inline pill chips and metric alignment.

## Elevation & Depth

To reinforce the industrial aesthetic, depth is communicated primarily through **sturdy structural borders and layered tonal planes**, rather than diffuse drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiering**:
  - `Base`: `#F8FAFC` (Canvas and background).
  - `Layer 1`: `#FFFFFF` (Primary functional cards and content sheets), framed with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1` border.
  - `Layer 2 (Inverted / Control Surfaces)`: `#0B192C` with internal borders of `#1E293B`.
- **Shadows**:
  - **Flat State**: `none`, relying strictly on `1px` to `2px` slate steel outlines (`#E2E8F0` on light, `#1E293B` on dark).
  - **Elevated Interactive / Hover**: Hard, crisp architectural offset shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06)`.
  - **Floating Modal / Overlay**: Deep structural drop shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.18), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft / Architectural (Level 1)** shape language (`roundedness: 1`). 

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges)**: Fixed `0.25rem` (4px) border-radius. This avoids blunt brutality while signaling industrial durability, engineered precision, and structural steel geometry.
- **Panels & Cards**: `0.5rem` (8px) border-radius (`rounded-lg`), grounding large surfaces without softening their professional edge.
- **Pills / Status Dots**: Strictly reserved for active system status badges and fleet tracking indicators where circular geometry signals real-time mechanical state.
- **Borders**: Dominant throughout the system. Outer framing borders maintain an uncompromising `1px` or `2px` stroke weight to provide architectural containment for complex transport data.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#EA580C`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `4px`, font `Chivo 600`, 0.02em letter spacing, uppercase. Hover shifts to `#C2410C` with a crisp 1px `#9A3412` bottom bevel effect.
- **Secondary / Fleet**: Background `#0B192C`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #1E293B`. Hover states lighten background to `#1E293B`.
- **Outline / Utility**: Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #334155`, text `#0B192C`. Hover fills with `#F1F5F9`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0B192C`, radius `4px`. On focus, transition to `2px solid #0B192C` with a 2px offset safety amber halo (`#F59E0B`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Square 18px boxes with rigid 2px corners (`roundedness: 1`), solid `#0B192C` active fill with white checkmark glyph.

### Cards & Panels
- **Standard Card**: White background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `8px` corner radius. Header features an optional dark navy top strip (`3px solid #0B192C`) to anchor visual weight.
- **Industrial Metric Card**: Dark theme variant with `#0B192C` background, `#1E293B` borders, displaying high-contrast monospaced telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`) with amber indicator accents.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Equipment & Load Status**: Height 24px, radius `4px`, padding `2px 8px`. Typography `JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase`.
  - *Dispatched / En Route*: Light amber tint background (`#FEF3C7`), text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FCD34D`.
  - *Delivered / Cleared*: Light emerald tint background (`#ECFDF5`), text `#047857`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
  - *Heavy Haul / Oversize Alert*: Deep navy background (`#0B192C`), text `#EA580C`, border `1px solid #EA580C`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Freight Specification Matrix**: High-density key-value data table featuring alternating `#F8FAFC` and `#FFFFFF` row fills, `1px solid #E2E8F0` separators, and right-aligned monospaced dimension/weight metrics.
- **Route Clearance Indicator**: Segmented bar utilizing amber, navy, and slate to convey axle-weight distribution and multi-state escort requirements.
